Your fan is three feet away. Turning it on shouldn't mean finding your phone, unlocking it, waiting for an app to load, and tapping through a screen.

Windbar puts your smart fans in the macOS menu bar. Click the icon, your fans are there. Click again to turn one off. Set a keyboard shortcut and you never have to click at all.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Power, speed, oscillation and mode, from the menu bar
• A global keyboard shortcut that toggles your last-used fan from anywhere
• A shortcut per fan, so one key hits the bedroom and another hits the office
• The fiddly per-device settings too, like Child Lock and display brightness
• Pair a brand new fan onto your WiFi over Bluetooth, with no phone involved
• Offline fans are shown as offline, instead of pretending to work

BUILT FOR A KEYBOARD

Every control is reachable from a URL, so Shortcuts, Stream Deck, Keyboard Maestro, a foot pedal or any macro key can drive your fans. Copy a fan's trigger link straight from its menu.

THE CONTROLS DRAW THEMSELVES

Windbar reads what each fan reports it can do and builds the interface from that. A tower fan and an air circulator both work without model-specific code, and newer models that ship their layout in the vendor app are covered by a bundled description of 84 models.

NO DOCK ICON, NO WINDOW

It lives in the menu bar and nowhere else. It is sandboxed, it stores your password in the macOS Keychain, and it contains no analytics, no telemetry and no ads.

WORKS WITH DREO SMART FANS

Windbar is an independent project. It is not made by, endorsed by, or affiliated with Dreo. You need an existing Dreo account, and that account must have a real password: if you signed up with "Continue with Google" or "Sign in with Apple", the sign-in this app relies on will not work.

Requires macOS 14 or later.

Free and open source. The full source, including the first public write-up of how Dreo's Bluetooth pairing actually works, is at github.com/lucid-fabrics/dreo-menubar
